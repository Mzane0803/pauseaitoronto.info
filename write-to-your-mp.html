Write to your MP

A short, polite note from a constituent is one of the most direct ways to be heard. It takes five minutes.

What your letter asks

That Canada propose an international treaty prohibiting the development of superintelligence until there is broad scientific consensus that it can be done safely and controllably, and strong public support.

Most Canadians are already uneasy about where AI is heading, but MPs rarely hear from them. In May 2026, 68% of Canadians told the Angus Reid Institute that governments must heavily regulate AI and tech companies, even if it slows development.
